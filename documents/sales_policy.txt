Sales and Returns Policy

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Products must be unused and returned with original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection.

Damaged products should be reported within 48 hours of delivery with an order number and photographs. The support team reviews damaged-item claims within 2 business days.

Bulk orders of 20 or more units may qualify for a negotiated discount. Discounts require manager approval and must be recorded on the customer order.

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metropolitan areas and 5 to 8 business days in other serviceable locations.
